Privacy Policy — Capy Drop

Effective date: July 12, 2026

Capy Drop is developed by Kaqui Interactive (“we”, “us”).

Data collection

Capy Drop does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or any other data. The game:

  • requires no account, login, or registration;
  • works fully offline and makes no network connections;
  • contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs;
  • requests no device permissions.

Game progress (high score, settings, and your critter collection) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We have no access to it.

Children

Capy Drop does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes any of the above (for example, by adding optional ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the in-store data safety declarations will be updated accordingly.
